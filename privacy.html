Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

KoreMesh ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

KoreMesh is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect no personal information from you in order to use the app.

  • Data you create locally: Accounts, categories, budgets, savings goals, and transactions you enter are stored only on your device.
  • Receipt images: Photos you attach to transactions are stored locally on your device and never uploaded.
  • No account required: KoreMesh does not require registration, login, or any personally identifiable information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or contact information.
  • Financial account credentials, bank logins, or card numbers.
  • Location data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device fingerprints.
  • Analytics about how you use the app.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you create in KoreMesh is stored locally on your iPhone using the iOS file system and standard iOS security protections. We do not operate any servers that store or process your financial information. Because your data lives on your device, you are responsible for keeping a backup through iTunes/Finder or iCloud device backups if desired.

4. Third-Party Services

KoreMesh does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app runs entirely offline and does not communicate with any external server.

5. Children's Privacy

KoreMesh is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because we collect nothing from anyone, this policy applies uniformly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of KoreMesh after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: All your data is visible inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: Deleting KoreMesh from your device permanently removes all associated data.
  • Export: Use your device's standard backup tools to export your data.
