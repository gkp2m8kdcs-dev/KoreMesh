Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All KoreMesh data is stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload anything to a server, and we do not have access to your financial information.

How do I back up my data?

Use the standard iTunes/Finder or iCloud backup process to back up your device — your KoreMesh data is included automatically.

Can I use KoreMesh on multiple devices?

Currently KoreMesh is a single-device app. Your data stays on the device where you created it.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the KoreMesh app from your iPhone permanently removes all associated data. There is no separate "clear data" option because nothing is stored off-device.

Does KoreMesh connect to my bank?

No. KoreMesh is a manual tracker. You record transactions yourself, which keeps your banking credentials 100% safe and private.
